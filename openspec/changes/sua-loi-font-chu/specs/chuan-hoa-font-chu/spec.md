## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sua loi ky tu tieng Viet tren UI
He thong SHALL hien thi dung dau tieng Viet tren tat ca text UI trong UIUX.

#### Scenario: Hien thi text dung dau
- **WHEN** mo bat ky form UIUX
- **THEN** cac label/button/thong bao hien thi dung dau tieng Viet

### Requirement: Chuan hoa font hien thi
He thong SHALL ap dung font thong nhat tren toan bo giao dien UIUX.

#### Scenario: Font dong nhat
- **WHEN** mo cac form UI
- **THEN** font hien thi thong nhat theo cau hinh chung

### Requirement: Luu file voi UTF-8
He thong SHALL luu cac file UIUX chua tieng Viet voi ma hoa UTF-8.

#### Scenario: Khong con mojibake
- **WHEN** chinh sua va luu file UIUX
- **THEN** text tieng Viet khong bi loi ky tu
