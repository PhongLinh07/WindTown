## Muc tieu

Xac nhan loi `System.IO.FileLoadException` tai `frmLogin` da duoc khac phuc sau khi them bindingRedirect.

## Tien dieu kien

- Build solution thanh cong.
- Co day du package `System.Resources.Extensions` trong `packages`.

## Buoc thuc hien

1. Build solution `WindTown_VB`.
2. Chay ung dung va mo `frmLogin`.

## Ket qua mong doi

- Ung dung khong bi vo khi mo `frmLogin`.
- Khong xuat hien `System.IO.FileLoadException` ve `System.Resources.Extensions`.

## Ket qua thuc te

- Da build thanh cong (Debug|Any CPU). Chua the xac nhan runtime frmLogin trong moi truong nay.
